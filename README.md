# AISpeak — interactive prototype

Interactive prototype of **AISpeak**, a concept for an AI-powered English learning app.
Concept and design: [Aleksandra Novikova](https://www.aleksandra-novikova.com/ai-speak-app-concept).

**Live demo:** https://aleksandra-novikova.github.io/aispeak-prototype/

## What you can try

- Sign up and answer the onboarding questions (native language, goal, level, interests)
- Read an article and tap underlined words to see their meaning (EN / DE) and save them to your wordbank
- Watch a video lesson and take a short quiz
- Have a voice role-play with the AI Assistant (job interview, small talk, doctor's visit, directions) and get an assessment
- Check your progress, statistics and wordbank in the profile, and switch between dark and light themes

This is a design prototype: AI replies are scripted, nothing is recorded, and no data is stored or sent anywhere.
